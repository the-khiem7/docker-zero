Round 1: Round 1: whale passing the zero

2026-10-22Chosen: Horizon, Orca

Every direction combines a whale (the animal, never Docker's container whale) with a complete circle (the zero). Each sheet shows the app icon tile, the bare mark on light and dark, and the tile and mark at taskbar sizes. Rough sketches: shapes and colours are starting points, and any mark can take any palette. Pick one or mix several (for example 'shape of 6, colours of 9').

Choice note: Horizon sun idea + Orca whale and red ring

1

Breach

The whale leaps through the zero: its head passes in front of the ring, its tail stays behind it.

Strengths
Reads as motion and as 'passing through'; the woven overlap is the signature move.
Risks
A diagonal whale is close in spirit to many 'jumping whale' marks.
Known flaws
Tail detail blurs at 16 px.
2

Orbit

The zero is a full orbit; a small whale swims along it, leaving a three-dot wake.

Strengths
The ring stays perfect and dominant, so it reads as 0 first.
Risks
Whale is small; the mark can read as a planet with a moon.
Known flaws
Whale vanishes below 24 px.
3

Cut-out

A solid disc is the zero; the whale is cut out of it as negative space.

Strengths
One shape, one colour: strongest at tray size, easy to recolour for states.
Risks
The circle is a disc, not a ring.
Known flaws
Thin tail flukes close up at 16 px.
4

Dive

Inside the ring the whale dives: only the tail flukes stand above the water line.

Strengths
The flukes are unmistakably a whale and nothing like the side-view container whale.
Risks
Static rather than passing through.
Known flaws

5

Bubble ring

Strengths
Real whale behaviour gives the ring a story; dotted ring is distinctive.
Risks
Dots read as a loading spinner.
Known flaws

6

Known flaws
